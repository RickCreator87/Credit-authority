# RickCreator87 Credit Authority (RCCA)

## 📜 Overview
The **RickCreator87 Credit Authority** serves as the definitive root of truth for all personal lending activities, identity verification, and financial governance within the GitDigital ecosystem. 

This repository establishes the legal and technical framework for **Richard Duane Kindler's** lending authority, risk profiles, and compliance standards. It ensures that every transaction is backed by verified identity and adheres to federal and state tax obligations.

---

## 🛠 Core Functions
* **Identity Verification (KYC/AML):** Integration points for on-chain and off-chain identity verification.
* **Lending Governance:** Hardcoded rules for loan structures, interest rates, and repayment terms.
* **Compliance Framework:** Strict adherence to RWA (Real-World Asset) issuance standards.
* **Tax-First Logic:** Automated record-keeping for tax reporting and liability tracking.

## 🏗 Repository Structure
* `/compliance`: Regulatory documents and AML/KYC templates.
* `/governance`: Rules governing lending capacity and risk assessment.
* `/contracts`: (Optional) Smart contract hooks for Solana/Ethereum integration.
* `index.html`: The public-facing transparency dashboard.

## ⚖️ Legal Disclaimer
The RickCreator87 Credit Authority is a private administrative framework. All lending activities are subject to local financial regulations and internal GitDigital governance protocols.
